A beginners guide to

⚽ FOOTBALL ⚽



Football is a family of team sports where two teams try to move a ball down a field to score points by getting it into a goal or across a goal line.

Things required:

Basic Techniques

  • Footwork for careful handling of the ball
  • Agility for quick movement and dodging
  • Kicking for passing or scoring a goal

Rules

  • Avoid stepping out of bounds
  • Don't beat up people
  • Don't hold the ball with your hands

Physical Capabilities

  • Endurance to withstand high intensity training and real games
  • Speed
